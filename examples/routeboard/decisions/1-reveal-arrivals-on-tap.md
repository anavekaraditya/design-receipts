# Decision 1 — Reveal arrivals on tap

Date: 2026-11-05
Status: accepted
AI roles: Proposed by AI, Overruled by human

## Context

Routeboard helps riders find their stop before they check its arrivals. The route list needs to stay easy to scan.

## Decision

Show arrivals when a rider taps a stop.

## Options considered

- Chosen: Reveal arrivals for a stop when tapped.
- Alternative: Show arrival predictions under every stop.

## Rejected options and why

Inline arrivals compete with the stop names riders are scanning for.

## Trade-offs accepted

Checking the next arrival takes one additional tap.
